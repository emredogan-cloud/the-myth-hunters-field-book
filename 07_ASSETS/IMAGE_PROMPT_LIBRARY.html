2 · Bütün promptlarda geçen gövde

Her prompt bu gövdeyle başlar. Gövde gravür dilini kurar ve World Myths'le raf akrabalığını sağlar (BRIEF § 6.4).

Black and white line engraving, 19th-century natural-history plate style. Clean hairline cross-hatching, no grey wash, no colour, no shading gradients. Pure white background. High contrast, printable at 300 dpi on uncoated paper. Even, open composition with generous empty space for a child to write in. No text, no letters, no numerals anywhere in the image. No people's faces. No modern objects. No logos, no watermarks, no signature.
"No text" neden mutlak: üretilen harfler neredeyse her zaman bozuk çıkar ve bu kitapta harf bir süs değil bir cevaptır. Bütün yazı ve rakamlar dizgide eklenir, görselde değil.

3 · Sınıf promptları

cipher-key Şifre anahtarı levhası

[GÖVDE] + An empty reference grid of 5 columns by 4 rows, drawn as thin ruled boxes with a wider left-hand column for the key symbols. Each cell is blank and large enough to hold one hand-drawn mark. A narrow decorative border of simple repeated notches frames the grid. The grid sits in the upper half of the plate; the lower half is empty ruled writing space.

observation-plate Gözlem levhası

[GÖVDE] + A single specimen drawn centred as a natural-history plate: [NESNE]. Six thin leader lines run from six distinct parts of the specimen outward to empty label boxes at the plate edge. The specimen is drawn accurately enough that each labelled part is clearly distinguishable from the others.

sorting-grid Tasnif ızgarası

[GÖVDE] + Two vertical columns of four empty framed panels each, separated by a wide blank channel down the middle of the page for the reader to draw matching lines across. Each panel is an empty engraved cartouche with a simple ruled border. No content inside the panels.

region-map Bölge haritası

[GÖVDE] + An antique engraved map of [BÖLGE COĞRAFYASI], showing coastline, rivers and relief hachures only. Exactly four small empty circles mark points to be labelled by the reader. A compass rose in one corner and a plain scale bar. Nothing is named on the map itself.

writing-frame Çizim ve yazma çerçevesi

[GÖVDE] + A large empty rectangular drawing panel with a fine double-ruled engraved border, occupying the upper two thirds of the page. Below it, eight evenly spaced horizontal ruled writing lines with comfortable spacing for a child's handwriting. The panel interior is completely empty.

seal-stamp Bölge mührü

[GÖVDE] + A circular engraved stamp emblem, [MÜHÜR MOTİFİ], drawn as an empty outline badge. The rim carries [N] evenly spaced notches cut into it, one of which is noticeably deeper than the others. The centre of the badge is completely empty so a reader can write inside it.

route-map Rota haritası

[GÖVDE] + An antique engraved world map on an equal-area projection, coastlines and relief hachures only, no borders and no place names. Six small empty medallion frames are spaced around the margin, connected to the map by thin dotted lines. All medallions are empty.

culture-vignette Kültür vinyeti

[GÖVDE] + A small engraved vignette of [MANZARA VEYA NESNE], drawn as a headpiece ornament about one third of the page width. Landscape, architecture, plant, animal or craft object only. No human figures. No ceremonial or ritual objects. No religious imagery.

4 · Kültür güvenliği — prompt düzeyinde

CULTURE_POLICY.md'nin altı yasak çerçevesi burada negatif prompt hâline gelir. Bu satır her kültür vinyetine eklenir:

Negative: ceremonial regalia, ritual objects, altars, shrines, masks worn by people, tattooed faces, sacred symbols, deity figures, tribal stereotype, feathered headdress cliché, fantasy pastiche, mixed-culture ornament, invented script, invented symbols.
